**Handwriting AI Studio** is a web-based handwriting generation engine that transforms digital text—such as responses from ChatGPT—into your own natural, custom handwriting. Trace your personal alphabet once, fine-tune the character geometry, and export clean handwritten notes in seconds.

🚀 **Try the live app now:** [my-handwriting-ai.vercel.app](https://my-handwriting-ai.vercel.app/)

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Custom Character Tracing:** Trace letters, numbers, and punctuation directly on the interactive canvas to digitize your handwriting style.
* **Granular Font & Spacing Controls:** Fine-tune letter spacing, word gaps, line bounce/jitter, ink thickness, slant, and individual letter scaling for realistic variance.
* **Cross-Device Cloud Sync:** Authenticate with Google or Email to sync your handwriting glyphs across devices using Firebase Cloud Database.
* **Instant Clipboard Copy & Export:** Copy transparent PNGs directly to your clipboard to paste into Word, Google Docs, or download high-resolution images.
* **Responsive Lined Paper Render:** Canvas engine renders natural baseline offsets and paper lines automatically.
